Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if my daily QR code expires? expand_more

Daily QR tokens automatically regenerate every 120 seconds for geofence verification. If an old code was scanned or timed out, request a fresh scan from your workplace supervisor’s portal or log the session as an offline attendance with GPS timestamp.

How do I request an excused absence? expand_more

Go to Logbook > Request Exemption. Submit supporting documentation (such as a medical slip or industrial visit voucher) at least 24 hours in advance to alert your academic liaison.

When does the weekly logbook lock? expand_more

Every Friday at 11:59 PM local university time. Afterward, entries can only be unlocked with a written petition approved by your department attachment coordinator.
